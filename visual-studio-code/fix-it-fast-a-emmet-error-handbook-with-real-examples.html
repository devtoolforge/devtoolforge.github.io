What Is Emmet?

The Mechanics of Emmet

Common Emmet Mistakes

The same failure patterns show up again and again. Here are the four we meet most often, including the classic: Fighting emmet in files where abbreviations look like plain text.

  • Optimizing before measuring, then optimizing again after the metrics disagree.
  • Copying configuration from an old project without understanding what it does today.
  • Letting local settings drift from CI until nobody knows which one is right.
  • Assuming the default configuration matches your project's real requirements.

Every one of these fixes is cheap when caught early and expensive when caught late. A quarterly review of emmet — twenty minutes, honestly — catches most of them before they metastasize.

Advanced Tips
