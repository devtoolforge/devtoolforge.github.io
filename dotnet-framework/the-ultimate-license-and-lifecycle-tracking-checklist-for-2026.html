Best Practices for License and lifecycle tracking

These are the practices we see on teams where this area simply never causes drama:

  • Test the failure path, not just the happy path — most real incidents live there.

  • Start every project with a documented license and lifecycle tracking setup so the whole team follows the same conventions from day one.
