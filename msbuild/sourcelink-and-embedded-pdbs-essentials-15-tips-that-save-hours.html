What Is Sourcelink and embedded pdbs?

Sourcelink and embedded pdbs, in one sentence: portable PDBs mapping to source control. In practice that means the details are worth getting right once, because everything downstream inherits the decision. Everything below assumes the current 2026 toolchain, but the reasoning generalizes to older versions too.

The mental model is simple: one clear definition, one clear workflow, and configuration that matches reality. Most confusion around sourcelink and embedded pdbs comes from mixing those layers — mixing definitions from different eras, or configs copied from projects with different needs. Quick Example

Here is a compact, real-world xml example you can adapt directly. It shows the configuration style we recommend for sourcelink and embedded pdbs: explicit, commented where it matters, and free of magic.

<Target Name="StampBuild" BeforeTargets="Build">
  <PropertyGroup>
    <BuildStamp>$([System.DateTime]::Now.ToString(yyyyMMddHHmmss))</BuildStamp>
  </PropertyGroup>
  <WriteLinesToFile File="$(OutputPath)stamp.txt" Lines="Built at $(BuildStamp)" Overwrite="true" />
</Target>

Copy it, run it once, then trim it to what your project actually needs. The worst version of any snippet is the one nobody on the team fully understands.
