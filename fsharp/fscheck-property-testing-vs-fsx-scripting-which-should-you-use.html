What Is Fscheck property testing?

Fscheck property testing, in one sentence: generative tests asserting properties over random inputs. In practice that means the details are worth getting right once, because everything downstream inherits the decision. The mental model is simple: one clear definition, one clear workflow, and configuration that matches reality. Why Bother with Fscheck property testing?

"Why bother" is a fair question for any tooling topic. Fscheck property testing earns its keep in three concrete ways:

  • It cuts repetitive work out of the daily loop, which is where most of the hidden cost of tooling lives.
  • It turns tribal knowledge into something a newcomer can read, run, and verify.

Notice what is missing from that list: fashion. None of these benefits expire with a release cycle, which is why fscheck property testing keeps showing up in every serious F# conversation. It is one of the few practices that pays for itself within the first week of consistent use.

Common Fscheck property testing Mistakes

The same failure patterns show up again and again. Here are the four we meet most often, including the classic: Weak generators that never hit interesting edge cases.

  • Treating fscheck property testing as a one-time setup instead of a living part of the workflow.
  • Shipping changes without a rollback plan and improvising during the incident.
  • Letting local settings drift from CI until nobody knows which one is right.
