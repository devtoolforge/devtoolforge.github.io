What Is Cancellation tokens?

Cancellation tokens, in one sentence: cooperative cancellation across async operations. In practice that means the details are worth getting right once, because everything downstream inherits the decision. The key is treating it as a living part of the workflow rather than a one-time setup task.

The mental model is simple: one clear definition, one clear workflow, and configuration that matches reality. Most confusion around cancellation tokens comes from mixing those layers — mixing definitions from different eras, or configs copied from projects with different needs. Keep them separate and the rest of this guide follows naturally.

Troubleshooting Cancellation tokens

When things break, symptoms cluster into a handful of patterns. Match yours here and jump straight to the fix:

  • Performance collapsed after an upgrade — bisect the upgrade by rolling back one component at a time and re-measuring
  • It fails intermittently under load — look for shared state and timing assumptions that only break when things overlap
  • It fails only in CI but passes locally — compare build modes and clean versus incremental state; CI reveals stale-output bugs instantly

If none of these match, capture a full log before changing anything. For cancellation tokens that log almost always names the failing layer, and the fix becomes obvious instead of guessed.
