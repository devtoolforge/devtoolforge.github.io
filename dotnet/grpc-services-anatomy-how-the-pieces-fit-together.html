What Is Grpc services?

Grpc services, in one sentence: contract-first RPC over HTTP/2 with protobuf. In practice that means the details are worth getting right once, because everything downstream inherits the decision. Everything below assumes the current 2026 toolchain, but the reasoning generalizes to older versions too.

The mental model is simple: one clear definition, one clear workflow, and configuration that matches reality. Most confusion around grpc services comes from mixing those layers — mixing definitions from different eras, or configs copied from projects with different needs. Keep them separate and the rest of this guide follows naturally.

Grpc services vs Side-by-side runtimes

They overlap enough that the question is usually about trade-offs rather than right and wrong. Grpc services optimizes for typed; Side-by-side runtimes pushes harder on the opposite axis. List your constraints honestly — team experience, platform targets, and maintenance budget — and the answer usually picks itself.

Start from the problem, not the tool. If your pain is typed, Grpc services is the more direct route; if your team already lives in the other ecosystem, Side-by-side runtimes minimizes ramp-up cost. Whatever you choose, write down why, because the next person will ask.
