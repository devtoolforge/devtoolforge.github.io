What Is Typing promises and async?

Typing promises and async, in one sentence: Promise generics, awaited, and error shapes. In practice that means the details are worth getting right once, because everything downstream inherits the decision. The good news is that the fundamentals have been stable for years, so the effort you invest compounds instead of expiring with the next release.

The mental model is simple: one clear definition, one clear workflow, and configuration that matches reality. Most confusion around typing promises and async comes from mixing those layers — mixing definitions from different eras, or configs copied from projects with different needs. Why Bother with Typing promises and async?
