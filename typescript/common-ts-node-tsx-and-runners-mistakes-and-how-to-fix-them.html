Performance Considerations

Measure first. Capture baseline numbers for the scenario you care about before touching anything, because intuition about hot paths is famously wrong. For ts-node tsx and runners, that usually means timing the end-to-end operation, not the method you suspect, and recording memory alongside time. A ten-minute baseline turns a week of speculation into an afternoon of targeted work.

Change one thing at a time. Stacked optimizations hide which change actually helped and which added complexity for nothing. Troubleshooting Ts-node tsx and runners

When things break, symptoms cluster into a handful of patterns. Match yours here and jump straight to the fix:
